# SFT Model Comparison

| Question            | Base Model Answer           | Fine-Tuned Model Answer                                                   | Which is Better? | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High blood pressure | Rest and avoid stress.      | Reduce salt, stay active, manage stress, and follow prescribed medicines. | Fine-Tuned | More specific and safer. |
| Diabetes            | Diabetes needs treatment.   | Follow a balanced meal plan, regular movement, and clinician guidance.    | Fine-Tuned | More practical and domain-aware. |
| Asthma              | Use your inhaler if needed. | Avoid triggers, keep an inhaler ready, and seek help if breathing worsens. | Fine-Tuned | Clearer and more actionable. |
| Burn                | Seek help if serious.       | Cool with running water and cover with a clean dressing. | Fine-Tuned     | Includes first-aid steps. |
| Sleep               | Try sleeping more.          | Keep a regular bedtime and reduce screen time before bed. | Fine-Tuned    | Gives concrete habits. |
